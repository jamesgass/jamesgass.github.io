James Gass's webpage

I am an Honours student at the University of Sydney with interests in the intersection of arithmetic geometry and number theory. Recently, I have been bridging abstract theory with computations in Magma to explore finite field hypergeometric functions.

In my spare time I also love to travel and learn languages. So far, I have visited: Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Chile, Colombia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, France, Germany, Gibraltar, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Malta, Mexico, Morroco, Netherlands, Oman, Poland, Portugal, Romania, San Marino, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, The Bahamas, Tunisia, Türkiye, United Arab Emirates, United States of America, and the Vatican. In terms of languages, I have studied:

  • English (fluent);
  • Spanish (C1);
  • Persian (Farsi) (A2, mainly in reading);
  • French (A2 in reading, A1 in speaking).

I also play the drums and timbales in my spare time.
